# Executive Dashboard Charts

## 1. Revenue by City

### What does the chart show?

This chart compares the total revenue generated by each city and identifies the highest-performing markets.

### Why is it important?

It helps management understand which cities contribute the most to revenue, allowing marketing resources to be allocated more effectively.

### Business Decision

Increase advertising investment and marketing campaigns in high-performing cities such as Mashhad while evaluating improvement opportunities in lower-performing cities.

---

## 2. Customer Segments

### What does the chart show?

This chart displays the distribution of customers across different segments such as Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, and Needs Attention.

### Why is it important?

It provides a clear overview of customer behavior and helps management identify which groups require retention efforts and which groups offer growth opportunities.

### Business Decision

Reward Champions and Loyal Customers with loyalty programs while creating targeted campaigns to re-engage At Risk customers.

---

## 3. Average Spending by Discount Usage

### What does the chart show?

This chart compares the average spending of customers who used discounts with those who did not.

### Why is it important?

It helps evaluate whether discount campaigns are associated with higher customer spending and supports future promotional planning.

### Business Decision

If customers using discounts spend more on average, the company can optimize future promotional campaigns. If there is little difference, the discount strategy should be reviewed.
